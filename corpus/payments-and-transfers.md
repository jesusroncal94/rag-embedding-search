# Payments and Transfers

## SEPA Credit Transfer

A standard SEPA Credit Transfer sent before the cut-off time of 15:00 CET on a business day reaches the beneficiary bank on the next business day. Transfers submitted after the cut-off, at weekends or on TARGET2 holidays are processed on the following business day.

SEPA Credit Transfers are free of charge for personal accounts in euro. The maximum amount for a single SEPA Credit Transfer is 200000 EUR.

## SEPA Instant

SEPA Instant Credit Transfer settles in under ten seconds, at any hour, including weekends and holidays. The receiving bank must also support SEPA Instant; when it does not, the transfer falls back to a standard SEPA Credit Transfer and the customer is told before confirming.

The per-transaction limit for SEPA Instant is 100000 EUR. Instant transfers cannot be recalled once settled; a recall request can only be sent to the beneficiary bank, which is free to refuse it.

## Failed and returned transfers

A transfer is returned when the IBAN does not exist, the account is closed, or the beneficiary bank rejects it. Returned funds appear back on the account within two business days and the original reference is preserved so the customer can match the return to the outgoing payment.

An incorrect but valid IBAN belonging to another person cannot be reversed by the sending bank. The customer must request a recall, and the funds can only be returned if the unintended beneficiary agrees.

## Currency conversion

Payments in a currency other than euro are converted at the Mastercard wholesale rate applicable at the time of settlement, not at the time of purchase. Standard accounts pay a foreign exchange fee of 1.7 percent on card payments in foreign currency; ATM withdrawals in foreign currency carry the same fee.
