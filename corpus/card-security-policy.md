# Card Security Policy

## Blocking a card

A customer can block a debit card instantly from the mobile app under Card Settings. A blocked card stops all new card payments and ATM withdrawals within five seconds. Direct debits linked to the account are not affected by a card block and continue to be collected.

Blocking is reversible. The customer can unblock the same card from the app at any time, provided the card has not been permanently cancelled. A permanently cancelled card cannot be reactivated and a replacement must be ordered.

## Lost or stolen cards

If a card is lost or stolen the customer should block it in the app first and then order a replacement. A replacement card is produced within one business day and delivered within five to ten business days depending on the country. A virtual card is issued immediately and can be used for online payments and added to a mobile wallet while the physical card is in transit.

The card number of a replacement card is always new. Recurring subscriptions tied to the old card number stop working and must be updated by the customer.

## Suspected fraud

When the fraud monitoring system flags a transaction, the card is blocked automatically and the customer receives a push notification. The customer confirms or rejects the transaction in the app. If the customer confirms the transaction was genuine, the block is lifted automatically. If the customer rejects it, a dispute case is opened and the card stays blocked until a replacement is activated.

Support agents cannot unblock a card that was blocked by fraud monitoring. Only the customer can, and only by confirming the flagged transaction in the app.

## PIN and card limits

The card PIN can be viewed in the app after a strong customer authentication check. Daily payment and withdrawal limits can be changed in Card Settings. A limit increase above the standard ceiling of 2500 EUR per day requires identity re-verification and takes effect the next calendar day.
