# Account Opening and Verification

## Eligibility

An account can be opened by a resident of a supported country who is at least 18 years old and holds a supported identity document. One personal account per customer is allowed. A customer who already holds a closed account may reapply after 30 days.

## Identity verification

Identity verification is completed by video call or by photo-based document check, depending on the country of residence. The check normally completes in under ten minutes. When the automated check cannot read the document, the case is routed to a human reviewer and completes within one business day.

A verification attempt that fails three times locks the application for 24 hours. The customer can restart after that period with the same or a different identity document.

## Strong customer authentication

Under PSD2, strong customer authentication is required for online card payments, for adding a new transfer beneficiary, and for viewing the card PIN. Authentication combines the registered device with either a passcode or a biometric check.

Low-value contactless payments below 50 EUR may be exempt from strong customer authentication, but the exemption expires after five consecutive contactless payments or once a cumulative total of 150 EUR is reached, at which point the next payment requires a PIN.

## Closing an account

A customer can close the account from the app once the balance is zero and no disputes are open. Closure takes effect immediately and the IBAN is retired. Statements remain downloadable for ten years after closure, as required by record-keeping obligations.
